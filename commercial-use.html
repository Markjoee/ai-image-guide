Can You Use AI-Generated Images Commercially?

Sometimes—but never assume that every tool, plan or generated image has identical rights.

Check these six things before publishing

  1. Plan-specific terms: confirm whether your subscription tier permits the intended use.
  2. Output ownership and license: understand what rights the provider grants and what it does not guarantee.
  3. Reference uploads: make sure you have permission to upload photographs, artwork or client assets.
  4. Privacy: check whether generations are public, discoverable or used to improve services.
  5. Third-party rights: inspect trademarks, recognizable people, copyrighted characters and protected designs.
  6. Local rules: copyright and consumer-protection rules vary by jurisdiction and continue to evolve.
Important

This page is general information, not legal advice. For a client campaign, product packaging or other high-stakes use, review the current provider terms and consult a qualified lawyer.
