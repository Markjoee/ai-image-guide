How to Write Better AI Image Prompts

A useful prompt is a compact creative brief. Give the model a clear subject, setting, composition, visual style, lighting and constraints.

The six-part prompt formula

  1. Subject: what must appear?
  2. Action: what is happening?
  3. Setting: where is the scene?
  4. Composition: close-up, wide shot, top-down, centered?
  5. Style and light: editorial photo, watercolor, cinematic light?
  6. Constraints: aspect ratio, text to include, elements to avoid.

Example prompt

“A ceramic coffee cup on a light oak table beside a folded newspaper, morning sunlight entering from the left, calm minimalist café, close three-quarter view, warm neutral palette, premium lifestyle product photography, realistic materials, no people, no logos, landscape 4:3 composition.”

Improve results with one change at a time

Generate a first draft, identify the biggest problem, then request one focused edit. For example: “Keep the subject and camera angle unchanged, but make the background simpler and soften the shadows.” This makes it easier to understand which instruction helped.

Prompt checklist

  • Is the main subject unmistakable?
  • Have you described the camera angle or layout?
  • Does the visual style match the intended audience?
  • Have you specified exact text only when necessary?
  • Have you checked the result for malformed hands, objects, lettering and brand marks?
