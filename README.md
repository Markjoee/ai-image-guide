# AI Image Guide — launch package

This is a static, responsive English-language website starter.

## Before publishing
1. Choose and register a domain.
2. Replace every `YOUR-DOMAIN.com` with your real domain.
3. Replace `3873611095@qq.com` with a monitored email address.
4. Review and customize Privacy Policy and Terms for your business and jurisdiction.
5. Add your real author identity and document hands-on testing before claiming that a product was tested.
6. Apply to affiliate programs only after checking current eligibility. Do not add affiliate tracking links until accepted; disclose them clearly.
7. Upload the folder contents to a static host such as Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or GitHub Pages.
8. Verify the site on mobile, connect Google Search Console, submit `/sitemap.xml`, and check indexing.

## Important
The site is technically deployable as static HTML/CSS, but it is not yet publicly online. No domain, hosting account, email address, affiliate approval or Search Console access was supplied. Legal pages are starter templates, not legal advice. Search rankings and income are not guaranteed.
